## Executive Summary
This study examines employee attrition using a dataset of 870 employees to identify factors associated with employees leaving an organization and evaluate whether predictive modeling can help identify employees at risk of leaving. The goal is to use these findings to support more targeted and cost effective employee retention strategies.
Exploratory data analysis found that attrition was associated with several employee characteristics. Overtime was one of the strongest patterns, with employees working overtime experiencing a 31.7% attrition rate compared with 9.7% among employees who did not work overtime. Attrition was also higher among Sales Representatives (45.3%), employees with lower job involvement, and employees with shorter company and role tenure. Employees who left also tended to have lower monthly incomes. 
Two classification models, K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) and Naive Bayes, were evaluated using the same training and testing split. Using a 20% probability threshold, KNN achieved approximately 67% accuracy, 68% sensitivity, and 66% specificity, while Naive Bayes achieved approximately 75% accuracy, 76% sensitivity, and 75% specificity. Naive Bayes performed better across all three metrics, including identifying a greater proportion of employees who left.
A cost analysis further demonstrated the potential business value of attrition prediction. Under the study's assumptions of a $60,000 employee replacement cost and a $200 retention incentive per flagged employee, Naive Bayes produced an estimated total cost of $371,200, compared with $493,400 for KNN and $1.5 million when no predictive model was used. This represents estimated savings of $122,200 compared with KNN and $1.13 million compared with taking no action. 
Overall, the findings suggest that predictive modeling could help organizations focus retention efforts on employees at higher risk of leaving. A targeted retention pilot, particularly among Sales Representatives, could help determine whether these data-driven strategies translate into improved employee retention and measurable cost savings.
